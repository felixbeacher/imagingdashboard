Subtle Medical reports FDA clearance for CT image enhancement

United States · Company-reported FDA clearance

Subtle Medical reported FDA clearance for SubtleHD(CT), extending its image-enhancement portfolio into CT. The company describes software intended to reduce image noise and improve low-contrast detectability across CT systems.

What this means for you

For procurement teams, the next question is whether the cleared use matches your scanners, protocols and clinical tasks. Request the regulatory documentation and assess image quality locally before changing acquisition settings. For suppliers, cross-modality expansion is a competitive signal rather than evidence of adoption.

What to watch

The FDA decision record has not been independently matched in this pilot. Clearance should not be read as proof of better patient outcomes or permission to use the product in every jurisdiction.
