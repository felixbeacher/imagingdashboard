Subtle Medical adds local distribution in the UK and Italy

UK · Italy · Distribution agreements announced

Subtle Medical announced distribution agreements with AMG Medtech in the UK and Medical Horizons in Italy for its MRI and PET image-enhancement software. The company says the partners add local sales, implementation and support capacity.

What this means for you

For imaging providers, local implementation support is worth examining alongside the software itself. Ask who will train staff, resolve integration problems and support a mixed scanner fleet. Suppliers should watch whether distributor relationships translate into named deployments and repeat business.

What to watch

Look for named customer deployments and independently measured workflow results. A distribution agreement does not establish a hospital rollout or prove shorter waiting lists.
