Data coverage & interpretation
  • Reported values come from named sources; estimates retain their methodology and scope.
  • Registered research measures the pipeline of study activity; it is not completed clinical evidence, unique technologies or paid use.
  • FDA authorisations measure regulatory activity, not hospital adoption or the number of unique commercial products.
  • Consumer price indices are cost-pressure proxies; they do not directly measure hospital input costs.
  • Paid deployment, usage, renewals, reimbursement, funding and vendor revenues require separate sourced inputs. They are not inferred from the public benchmarks.
