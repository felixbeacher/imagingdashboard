Exact monthly scores and component definitions

GPR: geopolitical news attention. OFR: realised financial-market stress. GSCPI: global logistics pressure. Energy and food: nominal US-dollar commodity-price indices. Higher scores indicate more of the selected pressure.

Economic outlook

Consumer inflation describes general price changes. It is not healthcare-specific inflation or a measure of hospital input costs.

What to watch in medical imaging

Potential transmission channels—not measured effects on imaging demand or forecasts of vendor performance.

Purchasing budgets

Slower growth and tighter financing can constrain capital spending. Watch procurement decisions, replacement cycles and provider funding rather than treating a macro score as an equipment-sales forecast.

Equipment and delivery

Trade disruption and supply-chain pressure can affect components, shipping and service availability. Local sourcing and supplier exposure determine the impact on each modality.

Operating costs

Energy and inflation pressures can squeeze operating budgets. Consider local electricity prices, staffing, service contracts and currency movements; global food prices are broader background context.

How to read this page

Economic figures refresh with the dashboard build. The risk chart is a separate, dated snapshot: refreshing economic data does not advance its cutoff. Regional economic figures are selected-country medians, while the risk chart remains global.

Method, limitations and attribution

Each risk component is scored against a fixed January 2000–December 2025 reference of 312 monthly observations: 100 × (observations below the current value + half the ties) / 312. The composite averages the five scores; it is not itself a historical percentile. OFR daily observations are averaged by month. The supplied chart data have complete monthly coverage, with no interpolation or forward filling.

The calibration is retrospective, so this is not a real-time backtest. Energy and food indices are nominal, not inflation-adjusted. Equal weights are transparent but not empirically validated: correlated components can duplicate shocks, while calm components can dilute a regional crisis. Climate, cyber incidents, sovereign-default risk and currency instability have no dedicated components.
