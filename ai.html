Imaging AI · evidence and market context

Imaging AI includes tools for image analysis, prioritisation and clinical workflow support. Its usefulness depends on the intended task, validation in the relevant care setting, integration and a sustainable purchasing model. Regulatory authorisation, registered research and routine clinical adoption are different measures; the indicators below should be read separately.

European Union · EUDAMED device registrations

European device-register records are shown separately from US FDA authorisations. EUDAMED registration is not an approval decision or evidence of clinical benefit. CE conformity assessment and, where applicable, notified-body certification must be checked separately.

This selected imaging-AI watchlist uses exact product-name searches, not a complete census. One product can have several device identifiers; record counts are not product or approval counts. No match does not establish that a product lacks CE marking.
