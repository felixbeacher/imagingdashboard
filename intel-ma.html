GE HealthCare moves towards PET tracer supply with SOFIE deal

United States · Agreement announced · completion pending

GE HealthCare announced an agreement to acquire SOFIE Biosciences for $945 million in cash. The announcement describes a US PET radiopharmaceutical manufacturing network and an expected closing in the first half of 2027, subject to conditions including regulatory approvals.

What this means for you

For PET providers, tracer availability deserves attention alongside scanner capacity. Examine local delivery coverage, backup arrangements and supplier concentration. For imaging suppliers, the proposed combination suggests a closer commercial link between equipment and radiopharmaceutical infrastructure; its operational effects remain to be demonstrated.

What to watch

Track closing conditions and subsequent service arrangements. This is an announced transaction, not a completed acquisition.
