An example engagement

From a broad question to a useful brief.

Illustrative scope, rather than a completed client project.

Competitor intelligence / Imaging AI

Five companies.
One market.
A clearer picture.

A vendor considering UK expansion commissions a comparison of five relevant imaging-AI companies, followed by an agreed monthly update.

The aim: identify meaningful differences, potential partners and developments that merit further investigation.

  • 01

    Executive brief

    What changed, why it matters to the expansion question and what to investigate next.

  • 02

    Company comparison

    Intended applications, jurisdiction-specific regulatory status, published evidence and announced deployments.

  • 03

    Source register

    Links, dates and evidence gaps, with company claims distinguished from independent findings.

  • 04

    Discussion and next steps

    A review of the findings and a prioritised set of questions for commercial follow-up.

Before you enquire

How are projects priced?

Projects are quoted individually against an agreed scope. The fee and deliverables are confirmed before any paid work begins. A one-off brief is a starting point; recurring monitoring can be discussed separately.

How is this different from the free dashboard?

The dashboard provides a broad public overview. Commissioned research focuses on your selected companies, market and question, with agreed outputs and a discussion of the findings.

Which technologies and markets can a project cover?

The dashboard covers CT, MRI, PET, X-ray and imaging AI. Consultancy scope is agreed after checking the relevant sources and their geographical coverage. Public evidence is uneven, so feasibility and limitations are discussed at the outset.

What are the limits of the research?

Work is based on accessible, appropriately reusable sources and any separately agreed inputs. A project does not promise exhaustive coverage, proprietary sales data or access to confidential procurement plans. Clinical, regulatory, legal and investment decisions require the appropriate professional assessment.
